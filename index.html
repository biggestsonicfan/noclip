Model 2 — interactive 3D explorer

The viewer builds its regions from Sonic The Fighters' own program EPROMs — epr-19001–epr-19004 — and from the mask ROMs mpr-19005–mpr-19020, which MAME shares with the parent set, Sonic Championship (schamp). A split set keeps those two halves in separate zips, so a combined set is the easiest thing to drop:

  • Non-merged — sfight.zip on its own; it carries everything.
  • Split — sfight.zip and schamp.zip together: the program EPROMs come from the first, the data ROMs from the second.
  • Merged — schamp.zip on its own; the clone's EPROMs are inside it.

Sonic The Fighters
A motion id indexes offset_list_motions at 0x6400004. Its record holds 60 channels — 20 objects × 3 axes — of constant, linear or cubic-Hermite keyframes; twelve objects are joint angles and eight are the points the pose is solved towards. The rig is not a plain chain: the chest and head aim at a target, and each limb is a two-bone IK chain reaching for one, exactly as calc_rob_angle_cont drives the coprocessor. See Animation.
